# v0.4.0: Pick your challenge

Difficulty levels, a table of the best games, extra ships, hyperspace, a new kind of enemy, and sound to fly to.

## New
- **Difficulty:** pick Easy, Normal or Hard on the title screen with `Left` / `Right` (d-pad on a gamepad). The game remembers your choice.
  - **Normal** is v0.3.0's game.
  - **Easy:** fewer and slower rocks, a slower enemy that aims worse and comes less often, scouts only from wave 7, and an extra ship every 7,500 points.
  - **Hard:** more and faster rocks, a quicker and sharper enemy that takes four hits and comes more often, scouts from wave 3, a 2-second shield, and an extra ship every 15,000 points.
- **Best scores:** each difficulty keeps the ten best games, with name, score and wave.
  - A game that makes the table asks for your name (type it, then Enter; your last name is filled in).
  - The table shows after the game, with your row highlighted, and on the title screen, in turn with the controls.
  - Your best score from earlier versions moves into the Normal table as "PLAYER".
- **Extra ships:** every 10,000 points bring another ship (on Normal).
- **Hyperspace:** `E` (`B` or `LB` on a gamepad) jumps your ship to a random place in the arena and stops it. It gets you out of a tight spot, but you might land somewhere worse, and the next jump is 8 seconds away. The HUD shows when it's ready.
- **Scouts:** from wave 5 on, about half the enemy ships are scouts: small, yellow and quick. A scout turns often, aims worse and fires less, and half the time it sees your shot coming and swerves. One hit destroys it, for 1,500 points.
- **Enemies come sooner:** the wait between enemy ships gets 6% shorter with every wave, down to 40% of the first wave's.
- **Ambient sound:**
  - **Title:** a warm, slowly breathing synth pad with a soft, low arpeggio inside it.
  - **Game:** a dark drone with space wind, an engine hum that rises with your speed, and a low throb while an enemy ship is around.
  - A new **Ambience** slider in the options sets its volume.
- **Quitting:** `Q` in the pause menu or after a game goes back to the title screen; `Q` or `Esc` on the title screen quits the game.

## Fixed
- **Fullscreen on Windows:** switching to fullscreen could time out ("Waited 5000ms for ...") and leave the window as it was.
  - **At start**, with fullscreen saved in the options: the game now opens fullscreen from the start.
  - **During play**, with `F11` or the options: fixed in EngineNamedScratch v0.8.2, which this version uses.

## Tuning file
- **New values** for the new features: `ship.extraLifeEvery`, `hyperspace.cooldown`, `enemy.waitCutPerWave`, `scout.*` and `score.scoutDestroyed`. `tuning.example.properties` lists them all, with Easy's and Hard's values next to Normal's.
- **Difficulty:** a `tuning.properties` applies on top of the difficulty you pick. Values at their Normal default change nothing, so a file with the v0.3 defaults leaves Easy and Hard as they are and doesn't count as custom.

## Downloads
- **Linux, macOS, Windows:** `SimpleAstroids-0.4.0-<platform>` contains the game with its own Java runtime, so there's nothing to install.
  - Unpack it and start `bin/SimpleAstroids` (Linux), `SimpleAstroids.app` (macOS) or `SimpleAstroids.exe` (Windows).
  - The macOS build isn't signed by Apple, so macOS refuses it the first time ("can't be opened" or "is damaged"). Either go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway", or run this once in Terminal (adjust the path if the app isn't in Downloads):
    `xattr -cr ~/Downloads/SimpleAstroids.app`
- **Any system with Java 25:** `SimpleAstroids-0.4.0-java.zip`. Unpack it and run `java -jar simple-astroids.jar`.

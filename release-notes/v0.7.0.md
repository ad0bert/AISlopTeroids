# v0.7.0: Rocks that collide, a gun that runs out

SimpleAstroids is now **AISlopTeroids**. Rocks now knock into each other, your gun fires laser bolts from a battery that can run flat, and the scout is easier to hit.

## A new name
- The game is now called AISlopTeroids, and so are its downloads.
- Your settings, best scores and `tuning.properties` come along: the first time AISlopTeroids starts, it copies them from the old `simple-astroids` folder into its new `ai-slop-teroids` folder (see the README for where that is). The old folder is left as it was; delete it once you're happy.
- If you start the game with options, they're renamed too: `-Dastroids.autopilot` is now `-Daislopteroids.autopilot`, and so on.

## New
- **Rocks collide:** rocks that meet now knock together and bounce apart instead of passing through each other.
  - Rock isn't rubber: they lose most of their speed in the knock, scrape along each other on a glancing hit, and come away tumbling a new way.
  - A hard knock makes a dull thud and throws off a little grit.
- **Laser battery:** every shot takes a charge from your gun's battery, so you can't hold down fire forever.
  - A full battery holds 35 shots on Normal, 42 on Easy and 32 on Hard: about five seconds of steady fire.
  - It recharges only once you let go of fire for a moment. Tapping fire doesn't let it recharge.
  - When it's flat, the gun gives a dry buzz instead of a shot.
  - The charge shows as a LASER bar next to the jump readout, and as a bar of cells under the hyperspace bar on the cockpit glass. Both turn red as the battery runs low and blink once it's flat.

## Changed
- **Laser bolts:** your shots are now long, thin bolts with a white-hot core in an amber glow. The enemy's shots glow too, magenta around a bright core, and stay short and fat so they're easy to see and dodge.
- **Bigger scout:** the scout is a fifth bigger, so it's no longer nearly impossible to hit.
- New waves place their rocks clear of each other.

## Fixed
- After pausing with `P`, only `P` closed the pause screen. Now `P`, `Esc` or a click goes back to the game, however you paused it.

## For playtesting
- New keys in `tuning.properties`: `gun.battery`, `gun.recharge` and `gun.rechargeDelay` for the battery, and `rocks.bounce` and `rocks.friction` for how rocks bounce and scrape.

## Downloads
- **Linux, macOS, Windows:** `AISlopTeroids-0.7.0-<platform>` contains the game with its own Java runtime, so there's nothing to install.
  - Unpack it and start `bin/AISlopTeroids` (Linux), `AISlopTeroids.app` (macOS) or `AISlopTeroids.exe` (Windows).
  - The macOS build isn't signed by Apple, so macOS refuses it the first time ("can't be opened" or "is damaged"). Either go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway", or run this once in Terminal (adjust the path if the app isn't in Downloads):
    `xattr -cr ~/Downloads/AISlopTeroids.app`
- **Any system with Java 25:** `AISlopTeroids-0.7.0-java.zip`. Unpack it and run `java -jar ai-slop-teroids.jar`.

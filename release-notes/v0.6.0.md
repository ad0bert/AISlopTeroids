# v0.6.0: In the pilot's seat

You now sit in a glass cockpit, or watch your own ship from behind. The ship flies differently too: it turns with some weight, brakes instead of reversing, and keeps its speed for longer.

## New
- **Glass cockpit:** you look out through a bubble of clear glass that catches the light towards its edges, over a low, curved dashboard, with the frame's arch across the top corners and thin teal lines glowing along them.
  - Your speed (digits over a bar) and the hyperspace charge (a bar that pulses when the jump is ready) are lit on the glass above the dashboard, with a dashed ring round the crosshair.
- **Three views:** `V` (or `Y` on a gamepad) switches between the cockpit, the same seat with no cockpit, and the view from behind your ship. The options panel has the view too.
- **Your ship from behind:** a long teal fighter with a dark glass canopy and four chrome wings spread in an X, with a gun on each wingtip.
  - Its engine fires a bright flame that grows while you thrust.
  - Steering thrusters at the wingtips puff gas while you turn: turning right fires the left wing's, turning up the lower ones, and the other way round.
- **Shots from the guns:** your shots leave the guns (below the dashboard in the cockpit, at the four wingtips from behind) and close in on the crosshair. Where they hit is unchanged.
- **Watch your ship go:** when your ship is hit, the view pulls back and turns to watch it break up in fire, smoke and torn hull. The next ship brings you back to your view.
- **Graphics quality** in the options: Low, Medium or High, for slower computers. Lower levels make fewer pieces, sparks and puffs of smoke, and Low also turns off the glow, edge smoothing and the light of explosions.

## Changed
- **Flying:**
  - The engine pushes only ahead, so to fly somewhere you turn towards it. Sliding (`A` / `D`) and rising and sinking (`Space` / `Shift`) are gone.
  - `S` (or the down arrow, or the left stick pulled down) now brakes to a stop instead of pushing you backwards.
  - Once up to speed, the ship keeps drifting much longer when you let go of thrust.
  - The ship turns after the mouse with a little inertia, settling where you point.
- **Easier-to-read text:** all text is sharp at every size and grows with the window, so fullscreen no longer means small text on a big screen. The title screen shows the controls as a table on a dark backing, and the options panel has larger text.

## Fixed
- Opening the options panel could change a setting, such as the graphics quality, when the pointer was over it.

## Downloads
- **Linux, macOS, Windows:** `SimpleAstroids-0.6.0-<platform>` contains the game with its own Java runtime, so there's nothing to install.
  - Unpack it and start `bin/SimpleAstroids` (Linux), `SimpleAstroids.app` (macOS) or `SimpleAstroids.exe` (Windows).
  - The macOS build isn't signed by Apple, so macOS refuses it the first time ("can't be opened" or "is damaged"). Either go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway", or run this once in Terminal (adjust the path if the app isn't in Downloads):
    `xattr -cr ~/Downloads/SimpleAstroids.app`
- **Any system with Java 25:** `SimpleAstroids-0.6.0-java.zip`. Unpack it and run `java -jar simple-astroids.jar`.

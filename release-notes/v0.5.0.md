# v0.5.0: Rock and metal

A visual update: asteroids that look like rock, enemy ships that look like machines, and things that break apart properly. The game plays the same, except that the enemy ships are a little bigger.

## New
- **Asteroids in twelve shapes:** every rock is lumpy and cratered, in one of twelve different shapes, and still stretched and tumbling at random. Each shape matches the rock's size for hits, so what you see is what you hit.
- **Rock surface:** a rock texture with grey and brown patches, darker hollows and small craters, and a bump map that makes the grain and craters catch the light.
- **Rocks break apart:** a hit rock breaks into sharp-edged chips of rock (many small ones, a few large) and a cloud of dust, instead of cubes. The pieces keep the rock's motion.
- **New enemy ships:** each is built from several parts.
  - **Enemy ship:** a polished metal saucer with a raised rim and a red band, a red glass dome, a ring of red and white lights, and a glowing engine underneath.
  - **Scout:** a flatter, sharp-edged hull in orange-gold paint, three swept chrome fins, a yellow canopy and yellow lights.
- **Shine and wear:**
  - The hulls reflect the space around them: the metal like a mirror, the paint and glass with a gloss.
  - They aren't new any more: they have plates and rivets, grime streaks, and scratches that catch the light.
- **Ships explode:** an enemy ship blows up in a flash of light, a ragged fireball that burns out into smoke, and torn pieces of its hull tumbling away, with embers streaking out. A hit that doesn't destroy it throws sparks and small pieces.

## Changed
- **Enemy ships are 20% bigger:** the enemy ship and the scout are easier to see, and to hit.
- **Space:** the background has a faint nebula and the soft blue light of a planet in one direction. The hulls reflect them, and the rocks get a cool blue light on their shadowed side.

## Downloads
- **Linux, macOS, Windows:** `SimpleAstroids-0.5.0-<platform>` contains the game with its own Java runtime, so there's nothing to install.
  - Unpack it and start `bin/SimpleAstroids` (Linux), `SimpleAstroids.app` (macOS) or `SimpleAstroids.exe` (Windows).
  - The macOS build isn't signed by Apple, so macOS refuses it the first time ("can't be opened" or "is damaged"). Either go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway", or run this once in Terminal (adjust the path if the app isn't in Downloads):
    `xattr -cr ~/Downloads/SimpleAstroids.app`
- **Any system with Java 25:** `SimpleAstroids-0.5.0-java.zip`. Unpack it and run `java -jar simple-astroids.jar`.
